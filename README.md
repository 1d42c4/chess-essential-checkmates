# Checkmate Without Stalemate

Book 08 of **Chess, One Decision at a Time**.

Cover the escape squares, protect the checking piece, and verify the final position.

[Read the full web edition](https://knightway8.github.io/chess-essential-checkmates/) · [Read the 12-page PDF](08_chess_essential_checkmates.pdf) · [Download the replayable PGN](positions.pgn)

## Three lessons

1. Queen and king close the cage
2. The king must help the rook
3. No legal move is not always mate

Each lesson includes a worked board, a position that changes an important detail, three questions, full answers, a failure case, and a transfer exercise. Read the model, solve before revealing, then explain the opponent's reply. Designed for players who already know piece movement.

## Contents and checking

- One 12-page PDF; full equivalent HTML text; nine SVG boards; six PGN starting positions.
- Editable lesson and position data in `source/lessons.json`, including depth-18 Stockfish 19 analysis and available exact tablebase results.
- Model sequences can be illustrative rather than forced. Legal or positional exercises may accept more than one answer; no engine score is presented as a general chess rule.
- [Credits and artwork source](CREDITS.md), [preservation details](PRESERVATION.md).

Open `index.html` locally or read on GitHub Pages; no build, account, external script, or tracking is required. To report a correction, give the lesson ID, FEN, move and proposed explanation in an issue. Preserve the published edition while reviewing corrections.
